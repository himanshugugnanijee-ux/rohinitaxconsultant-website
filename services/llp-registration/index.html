LLP Registration in Rohini, Delhi

Limited Liability Partnership incorporation with partner DSC, name approval and a properly drafted LLP agreement.

Call +91-7827788236

An LLP combines the flexibility of a partnership with limited liability for partners, and has lighter compliance than a company. It suits professional firms, family businesses and service ventures that do not plan to raise equity funding.

The LLP agreement is the document that matters most: it fixes profit share, capital, decision rights and exit terms. We draft it around what the partners have actually agreed, and file it within the time allowed after incorporation.

Who needs LLP Registration?

  • Two or more partners starting a service or trading business
  • Professionals practising together
  • Existing partnership firms that want limited liability
  • Businesses that prefer lower annual compliance than a company

Documents required

  • PAN and Aadhaar of partners
  • Photographs and address proof
  • Registered office proof with owner's NOC
  • Agreed capital contribution and profit-sharing ratio

Our process

  1. 1DSC for designated partners
  2. 2Name reservation
  3. 3Incorporation filing and certificate
  4. 4Drafting, stamping and filing of the LLP agreement

Questions about LLP Registration

How many partners are needed?

Minimum two designated partners, at least one of them resident in India. There is no upper limit.

Is audit compulsory for an LLP?

Only when turnover or contribution crosses the limits set in the LLP rules. Annual filings are required every year regardless.

What if the LLP agreement is filed late?

A per-day additional fee applies, so we file it soon after incorporation.
