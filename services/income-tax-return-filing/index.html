Income Tax Return Filing in Rohini, Delhi

ITR filing for salaried people, professionals, traders and senior citizens with the right form and every eligible deduction.

Filing an income tax return is more than uploading Form 16. The return has to match your Annual Information Statement, pick the correct ITR form, and choose between the old and new tax regime in the way that leaves you with the lowest legal tax. At Rohini Tax Consultant we prepare your computation first, show you the tax or refund figure, and file only after you approve it.

We file returns for salaried employees, pensioners, freelancers, shopkeepers, landlords and people with share-market or property gains. If you have missed earlier years, we also help with belated and updated returns where the law still allows them.

Who needs Income Tax Return Filing?

  • Salaried employees and pensioners with Form 16
  • Freelancers, doctors, consultants and other professionals
  • Shop owners and traders, including presumptive-income cases
  • Anyone with capital gains, rental income or foreign income
  • People who need ITR copies for a loan, visa or tender

Documents required

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Form 16 and salary slips
  • Bank statements and interest certificates
  • AIS / Form 26AS (we download it for you)
  • Investment, insurance and home-loan proofs
  • Sale and purchase details of shares, mutual funds or property

Questions about Income Tax Return Filing

Which ITR form applies to me?

It depends on your income sources. Salary-only cases usually fall under ITR-1, capital gains under ITR-2, and business or professional income under ITR-3 or ITR-4. We confirm the form after seeing your documents.

Can I file a return after the due date?

Yes, a belated return can generally be filed until 31 December of the assessment year with a late fee, and an updated return is possible for some earlier years. We check which option is open for you.

How long does a refund take?

Most refunds are credited within a few weeks of e-verification, provided your bank account is pre-validated and the return has no mismatch.
