Proprietorship Firm Setup in Rohini, Delhi

Start a sole proprietorship the right way with GST or Udyam, a current account and basic books in place.

Call +91-7827788236

A proprietorship has no single registration certificate. Its identity is built from the registrations it holds, such as GST, Udyam and a shop licence, plus a current account in the business name. Banks ask for at least two such proofs.

We work out which registrations your business really needs, obtain them, and set up a simple record-keeping method so that your first income tax return is easy.

Who needs Proprietorship Firm Setup?

  • First-time shop owners and home-based sellers
  • Freelancers who need a business name on invoices
  • Online sellers getting started on marketplaces

Documents required

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor
  • Photograph
  • Address proof of the business place
  • Bank details

Our process

  1. 1We list the registrations suited to your trade
  2. 2Applications are filed for GST, Udyam or other licences
  3. 3Certificates are used to open a current account
  4. 4Invoice format and bookkeeping are set up

Questions about Proprietorship Firm Setup

Is there a certificate of proprietorship?

No. GST registration, Udyam registration and similar licences together act as proof of the business.

Can I run a proprietorship from home?

Yes, most service and online businesses do.

How is a proprietor taxed?

Business profit is added to your personal income and taxed at slab rates in your own return.
